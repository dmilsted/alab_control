TEM Manual Grid Exposition

Instructions

Follow these steps to manually expose a TEM grid:

  1. Enter the exposure parameters below
  2. Click the "Prepare Head" button - this positions the needle and activates the vacuum
  3. Carefully place your TEM grid on the needle manually
  4. Click the "Expose Grid" button - this moves the grid to the charger, exposes it, and returns
  5. Carefully remove the exposed grid from the needle
  6. If you have more samples, place the new grid and click the "Expose Grid" button. If not, skip this step
  7. Click "Complete Procedure" to finish the process

⚠️ Caution: Handle grids with extreme care to prevent contamination or damage

Manual Control Procedure

Step 1: Prepare System for Grid Placement

Positions the needle and activates the vacuum system.

Step 2: Expose the Grid (or grids)

After placing the grid manually on the needle, click this button to move to charger and expose.


Note: If you have multiple grids, just change them after exposing and click on this button again for each grid.

Step 3: Complete the Procedure

After removing the grid from the needle, click to end the procedure and reset the system.
